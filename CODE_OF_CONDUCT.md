# Community Code of Conduct

Prompt Vault welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Critique ideas and changes without attacking people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and clear reasoning.
- Respect privacy; do not publish another person's personal or confidential information.
- Help keep discussions welcoming and focused on improving the project.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sharing private information without permission.
- Deliberately posting unsafe, misleading, or malicious content.
- Spam, repeated disruption, or attempts to derail project discussions.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit, reject, hide, or remove contributions that violate this code or the project's content guidelines. They may close discussions that cannot remain constructive.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns through the repository's issue tracker. Do not include private information or sensitive details in a public report. Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as possible.

This code applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
