# Content guidelines

Prompt Vault aims to publish original, practical, reusable resources.

## Quality
- Give each resource one clear purpose and a descriptive title.
- Explain when the resource is useful and include enough context to use it.
- Use clear placeholders such as [TOPIC] or [CONSTRAINTS] where input is required.
- Prefer actionable steps, testable outputs, and honest uncertainty.
- Avoid duplicate entries and claims that a prompt guarantees a result.

## Safety and privacy
- Do not request passwords, authentication codes, private keys, or unnecessary personal information.
- Do not include instructions that facilitate harm, abuse, illegal activity, or evasion of safety controls.
- Avoid collecting sensitive personal data. Use fictional examples or placeholders.
- For health, legal, financial, and other high-impact topics, require appropriate professional review and clearly communicate limitations.

## Copyright and attribution
- Submit original writing or material you have permission to share.
- Do not copy paid, private, or copyrighted prompt packs.
- Attribute factual frameworks and external references where appropriate.
- Do not submit song lyrics, book chapters, or other lengthy copyrighted passages.

## Metadata
- Use a unique lowercase kebab-case ID.
- Choose exactly one resource type: prompt or skill.
- Use a concise category and a few relevant tags.
- Choose beginner, intermediate, or advanced difficulty based on the amount of prior knowledge required.
- Select one or more existing curated collections that genuinely fit the resource.
- Set review_status to curated only after maintainer review; use pending for unreviewed suggestions and community for accepted community submissions.
- Use a valid YYYY-MM-DD date.
- Keep titles, descriptions, and content within the limits enforced by the catalogue validator.

Submissions may be edited or declined if they do not meet these standards.
